# TO WORLD / GERTECH — ART FOCUS: LIVING WORLDS + FAIRY INTERFACES
**Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Status:** Owner-supplied visual reference direction / priority art intake; not automatically Book canon or an instruction to publish private images.

## Immediate art target
The most recent owner direction is to focus new visual work **here**: a living creative universe, combining miniature tangible technology with impossible but physically legible worlds. The art collection is **reference**, not proof of executable game systems, VR passthrough, final 3D models, or finished comic pages. Do not substitute generic 169-page grid posters for real artwork.

**Dominant visual grammar:** The world is built—not summoned from nowhere. Sculpted articulated machines, modular crystalline geometry, translucent nebula/gummy-glass, gold/copper joinery, polished white or royal-blue armor, luminous blue/violet channels; rich miniature ecosystems of plants, mushrooms, circuitry and glass vessels. Give large vistas a sense of a lived-in place, and tiny desk/bowl pieces the same believable construction detail.

## Art emphasis by lane
1. **FAIRY / INTERFACE / SPAWN:** The fairy directs, observes and connects intent to a tangible network. The holo tabletop/spawn diagram and bowl environments are visual reference metaphors; do not claim a working network is depicted or that each AI automatically has a physical fairy. The fairy pilot can be a spark or crystal rather than a humanoid mascot. Where a humanoid is used, preserve age-appropriate adult anatomy; never infantilize adults.
2. **CRYSTAL-MECHA ANIMALS:** Preserve recognizable animal silhouettes, legible joints and material segmentation. Use compelling royal blue, silver/white, purple-energy and gold identities; rabbit twins, big cats, bull mounts, dragon-crabs and flyers belong to a related *engineering aesthetic*, not one identical reused creature.
3. **WORLDS / CRAFT / COMMUNITY:** Galaxy cubes and botanical stations are buildable-appearing modules; towns have work, shops, food, route safety, strangers, friends and humor; cosmic scale should not erase everyday human need or character relationships.
4. **BOWL / FOREST / MEMORY:** Realistic glass vessel and miniature forest with working bench, old electronics, message envelopes, data trails and fairy worker. A Memory Bank *bowl* is a visual metaphor distinct from a technical Atlas map, Codex or actual software cache.
5. **CINEMATIC HUMANS AND CREW:** Consistent human physical scale and character direction; clear adult roles, facial expressions and recognizably constructed equipment. Keep individual agent/creator aesthetics distinguishable; these references show a family of related looks, not a command that every story has identical colors.

## Essential boundaries and editorial gates
- **DO NOT** reroute TO FAIRY/Meta Quest 2 work into TO DOG/Godot. An art reference may later be turned into a verified `.glb` mesh; a JPG is not a usable scene asset.
- **DO NOT** make 169 index panels and claim 169 illustrated comic pages. A comic page requires a readable narrative, full panel compositions, readable balloons, consistent characters and verified page continuity.
- **DO NOT** automatically rewrite all existing black-and-white *Superhero Jail Janitor* pages in luminous fantasy colors. Noir is a story-specific established style; new cross-world chapters can use varied palettes while preserving story readability.
- **DO NOT** turn a generative crossover picture into literal canon, claim specific functioning technology from a render, or silently mix named IP/brands into the fictional world as commercial characters.
- **DO** preserve source filenames and originals; make derivative work separately with its own ID, role, parent source and approval status.
- **DO** let geometry communicate function and sound/motion/text back up color for accessibility. Human approval determines final character/canon promotion.
