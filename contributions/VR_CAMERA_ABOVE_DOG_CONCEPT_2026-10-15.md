# VR Camera Above DOG Concept Notes
**Date:** 2026-10-15
**Status:** Concept stage

## Core Idea
- Choose-your-own-adventure movie style experience.
- Heavy automation of routine interactions so the player can “just drive.”
- Meaningful choices still shape the adventure.
- Current viewpoint concept: In VR the player is the camera above DOG. DOG is the companion that acts in the world.
- Larger long-term ambition: modular interactive VR universe. Modularity is central.
- Cross-device roles are a future concept (device determines role). Examples only: VR = camera above DOG; Phone = flying fairy; Mouse+keyboard = rogue; Controller = tank.
- AI “overtake or assist” remains an open question.

## Open Questions
- How much direct influence the player has over DOG while remaining the elevated camera.

## Prototype Sketch
Short written interactive scene “First Corridor – Camera Above DOG” to test the viewpoint and low-click intent.
