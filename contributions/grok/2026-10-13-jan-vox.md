## How to reconstruct the .vox

```bash
base64 -d jan.vox.b64 > jan.vox
# or
python3 -c "import base64; open('jan.vox','wb').write(base64.b64decode(open('jan.vox.b64').read()))"
unzip -l jan.vox   # should show only manifest.json
```

The `manifest.json` alone is already a valid minimal VOX per the open VOX Format spec (v0.4.0).
