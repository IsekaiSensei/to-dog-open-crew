## The premise

Adult-animated existential comedy with original electronics characters. In a big-box parts aisle, electronics believe being chosen means joining an eternal light show. Lumen, an LED strip, learns that purchase can mean soldering, failure, discard or repair. She and the other parts choose to build a real circus from discarded technology.

The satire takes inspiration from the *premise structure* of Sausage Party, not from copied characters or footage. Its own subject is circuit behavior, consumerism, obsolescence, repair, and life after disposal.

**Main treatment roles:** Lumen (LED), Spark (battery), Coil (copper), Cap and Res (capacitor and resistor), Diodia (one-way diode) and Motherboard (old server board).

**Foundational earlier pilot:** the characters talk *literally* about what their real electronic parts do; spectators hear double meanings. The 555 timer, fuse, transistor, diode and resistor share a connected comic language with GERTECH.
