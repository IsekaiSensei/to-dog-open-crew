# Gertech shared foundations for Sierra

TO WORLD is the shared universe; Sierra's fairy game is her own expression of it. Terraria is a systems/reference inspiration. Terraria characters, art, music and source code are not included.

- A fairy and her own matching book belong together for pocket-dimension activity. Neither activates the pair alone.
- Crystal, ice and metal are established fairy-origin materials. Their implementation in Sierra's game remains hers to develop.
- World Seed emergence and encounter difficulty are different things. Fibonacci World Seed levels do not automatically number platforming stages or select a challenge die.
- Early actions stay useful as the world grows. Rock = Place; Paper = Inspect; Scissors = Develop/Remove; Void = Cancel/Wait/Clear/Preserve.
- The four physical domains are Mass, Motion, Heat/Energy and Fluid. Color says what; shape says what it does. No permanent element-to-color map is imposed by this pack.
- Natural wormholes precede engineered gates; mycelium senses them and mushrooms serve as interface nodes. A visual portal illustration is not proof a portal system is implemented.
- Preserve practical materials, tools and logistics. Existing shared foundations are not a requirement to recreate the retired fuse-room tutorial.
- Laika rule: crew return is prioritized. Make feedback understandable through labels, shape and sound as well as color.

Sierra chooses her game's title, fairy, starting location, traversal and interaction. New shared-world additions can be proposed without calling them settled canon. The workshop's practice items, recipe values, camera and scene layout are examples, not world rulings.

Source direction: Isekai Sensei's approved October 11 Sierra plan, later shared rules, and direct October 15–16 SD-card/Godot/2.5D corrections. Private source records remain private; no access to them is needed to read this handoff.
