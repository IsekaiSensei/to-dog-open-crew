## Layer 1 — Psyche 16 Starforge: five modular types

| Module | Primary role | Diagram subtitle |
|---|---|---|
| **Sucker** | Gripper, harvesting, gathering, intake | GRIPPER / INTAKE |
| **Thruster** | Movement, pushing, thrust | MOVEMENT |
| **Inhabitor** | Occupied/pilot node, AI or crew control | CREW / AI CONTROL |
| **Frame** | Physical support, modular connections, shape | STRUCTURE |
| **Power** | Energy feed, forge and stored/received energy | ENERGY / FORGE |

The same *finite vocabulary of building functions* composes or reconfigures into vehicles, robots, animals, dragons, ships, starforges and other forms. Objects are not separately canonized templates simply because they are drawn: designs emerge from arrangements, quantities, material rules, constraints, and behaviors. Five module **types** does not mean precisely five parts total. “128 modules” refers to one illustrated example of a pile, not a locked engine limit. Psyche 16 is a fictional setting / game design reference inspired by real asteroid **16 Psyche**; in-world star-tapping, warp plasma, tempered cacao glass, Dyson taps, and expanding-inside spaces are science-fantasy mechanics, not real-world engineering assertions. Real asteroid gravity is low but not zero.
