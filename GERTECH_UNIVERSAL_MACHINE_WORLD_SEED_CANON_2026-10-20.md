# GERTECH / TO WORLD — Universal Machine-to-World Seeds

**CANON — owner directive, Isekai Sensei, 2026-10-20**  
**Scope:** Design and narrative canon for TO WORLD / GERTECH, including TO DOG as the playable repair branch.  
**Implementation status:** Design canon; NOT evidence that automated object scanning, chip provenance extraction, procedural world generation, or public model import is implemented.

> "If you don't have a big machine, just throw in your toaster. But Elon Musk? He's gonna run a space rocket through his, and then we could all access it from GitHub." — Isekai Sensei, October 20, 2026

## The rule

**One underlying world-building language must accommodate an ordinary household toaster, a Creality Ender 3D printer, and a rocket-scale machine.** Access to expensive equipment must not be a prerequisite for creating a world. The scale and complexity of the *input* change; the principles of decomposition, pathways, repair, provenance, seeds, and collaboration remain.

A toaster seed is not a rocket seed; they are independently meaningful, forkable representations sharing a common grammar.

## Two linked, necessary representations — do not choose one

1. **Engineering view: Breadboards / Breadboard Forest.** Decompose a real object's known functions into components, subsystems, paths, inputs, outputs, and testable modular circuit or mechanical models. Educational breadboards represent appropriate circuitry, not a claim that every production IC can be literally replaced with discrete breadboard parts. Circuit pathways grow into an explorable Breadboard Forest.
2. **Cinematic view: Beautiful Seed.** Preserve the Gremlin-originated beautiful-seed interpretation as a condensed visual/mythic representation of the same source. The seed can unfold into fantastic worlds in Sensei's photographic-reality-with-impossible-events style.

Both views point to the **same source object, functional map, and provenance**. Neither replaces the other. Forest = readable engineering; seed = cinematic compression; characters and landscapes dramatize the component functions and connections.

## Actual machine functions are the laws of each generated world

- **Mass:** materials, structure, recovery, supply routes.
- **Motion:** mechanisms, motors, gears, force transmission.
- **Heat / Energy:** supplies, heaters, protection, conversion and cooling.
- **Fluid:** pumps, passages, pressure, flow and thermal transport where relevant.
- **Signals and timing** pass through the above apparatus and its electronics; don't mistake them for magic door keys.

A toaster may yield heating-element terrain, control circuitry, a spring-release mechanism and maintenance problems; a printer adds motion control, fabrication, heat and tool paths; an authorized rocket subsystem can present the same domain language at much higher complexity. The components and interconnections determine credible failures and repairs. Fantasy visualization does not erase their roles.

## Maker provenance weaves the Zodiac into machinery

**The maker changes the cultural representation of a part.** Record the actual part identifier and—when known—designer, manufacturer, assembly/manufacturing origin, and evidence. These are not always the same country. Don't invent origins from brand names or claim a Chinese-branded assembled machine contains exclusively Chinese-made components.

In TO WORLD, verified or deliberately fictionalized provenance can inform the animal/cultural motif: China invokes Chinese zodiac traditions; Japan can invoke Japanese *eto*; Taiwan can invoke locally interpreted zodiac traditions; India can inspire distinct Indian astrological/symbolic traditions. These are distinct traditions, not interchangeable twelve-animal charts. Use care, research and respect in each adaptation.

The starting Creality Ender 3 offers a **Chinese maker/world starting identity**, while verified individual components may introduce other lineages. New parts weave new histories into existing machines; local biome animals (for example Texas armadillos and rabbits) coexist with provenance-inspired roles rather than replacing them.

## Keep the electronic components and the TO DOG repair truth

- The existing **fuse** tutorial is foundational and must remain intact: DOG SNIFFs, finds the fuse, installs it in the FuseBox; the door responds to the restored supply path. The FuseBox, not the door, owns the fuse.
- **555 timers** may supply timing/pulse stories where they are part of the modeled circuit; **LM347** quad operational amplifiers can represent actual signal conditioning/amplification where applicable. Do not assert that every sampled machine contains these parts.
- Electronic components (resistors, capacitors, transistors, regulators, sensors, logic and traces) stay visible and functionally meaningful beneath machines operated by the Zodiac/fairies.
- **Navy 3-M / PMS maintenance-card mindset:** inspect, trace fault, consult procedure, gather parts/tools, repair, verify, and log evidence. Most machines need the **right functioning path**, not an arbitrary keycard.
- A receipt/log may record an event, repair, or time-based evidence. It is not a replacement for the fuse or a magic gate token.

## Shared seed and GitHub contract

Each shareable object-world seed should *aim to* carry:
- Source object and exact model/version, with licensing and attribution.
- Parts and provenance records labeled **verified / inferred / fictional / unknown**.
- A functional graph of energy, motion, mass, fluid and signal paths.
- Breadboard Forest representation and Beautiful Seed representation, cross-linked.
- Proposed character ecology and maker/culture logic.
- Maintenance/repair scenarios and, when present, real test receipts.
- Version, parent seed, fork history, and evidence of what has actually run.

**The original World Seed is immutable.** Revisions and remixes fork into new branches/rooms; preserve provenance and history. GitHub is the public exchange for *cleared* seeds, specs and examples that people and agents may inspect or fork. Publishing a seed does **not** automatically publish working software, clone a real piece of hardware, grant users access to restricted designs, or imply that an outside manufacturer participates.

**Illustrative, not a SpaceX partnership:** a hypothetical engineer with permission could publish a cleared model of a rocket subsystem using this grammar. Elon Musk's name in the owner's example describes extreme scale, not a confirmed participant or an authorization to distribute confidential rocket designs.

## Design guardrails and handoff

- **TO WORLD** is the parent; **TO DOG** is the repair/game branch. Do not rebuild physics, electronics simulation, or rendering unnecessarily; use the existing game engine where possible.
- Do not flatten this into “a cast of 12 animals” or “a new unrelated world for every object.” This is one woven architecture across scales.
- Public creative material may be shared as a Page; private Books, unlicensed schematics and private personal/project data remain private.
- Preserve the user-approved difference between **canon intent**, **tested mechanics**, and **proposed future capabilities**.
- Crew follow-up: Work/XO can propose the smallest independently testable toaster seed or breadboard module; QC requires a real artifact and evidence, not a beautiful image alone.

**Owner's core principle:** Anybody can begin with what they have. A toaster belongs in the same world-making system as a rocket.
