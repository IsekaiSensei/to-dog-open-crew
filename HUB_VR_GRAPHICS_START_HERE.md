## What new agents should understand from this page alone

**Hierarchy:** TO WORLD / GERTECH is the parent universe and shared seed architecture; TO DOG is a game branch; Starforge Concept Page 001 is a canonical *concept*; this graphics/VR package is proposed art and mixed-reality design, **not a completed mechanic**.

**Five optional Starforge types:**
- **Sucker / SCK** = grip, salvage, intake.
- **Thruster / THR** = movement, thrust.
- **Inhabitor / INH** = pilot, resident, control node.
- **Frame / FRM** = structure, chassis.
- **Power / PWR** = energy, forge / heart.

The five are *optional combinable types*, not five required parts or universal verbs. A single-type assembly may be a valid **composition**; it still needs physics and gameplay tests before claiming it works.

**The light pilot is not the shell.** A Spark Fairy can be a tiny luminous pilot; a Silver Shadow Fairy-shaped PWR+THR vessel is one proposed constructed appearance. Do not silently add a Frame or cockpit to satisfy an obsolete mandatory-parts checklist.

**First VR pass-through (MR-0):** Quest shows the **real room**; a small optional spark/pilot hovers comfortably, makes a soft distinctive `ting`, responds to one controller or supported hand input, and can be hidden/exited. This is a **proposed** test target. Real room mapping, raw camera access, actual object occlusion, spatial anchors, and cross-device network sync are **not** claimed. Later tabletop Starforge, TO DOG overlays, portal window and full-VR transitions are future proposals.

**Preserve the engine:** Use actual Godot 4.7/OpenXR features, nodes, physics and input. Do not invent redundant game-engine systems. Preserve original World Seed versions and provenance.

**Accessibility:** blue/violet/gold + distinctive shape and sound; no red-only status; no rapid flicker or room-obstructing glow; safe recenter/hide/exit.
