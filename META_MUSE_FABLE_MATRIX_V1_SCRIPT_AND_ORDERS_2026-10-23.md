- Matrix rows / fable masks: **Fox, Crow, Tortoise, Ant, Lion, Owl**.
- Matrix columns / AI-interaction principles: **Context, Constraints, Verification, Iteration, Boundaries, Authority**.
- One cell = one five-second micro-fable: one animal mask, one principle, one sentence, one visible point.
- **0.0–0.5 s:** silent first visual beat (lead-in). **0.5–1.0 s:** silent second beat (prop/gesture). **1.0–5.0 s:** the single dialogue sentence. The first two halves together are **one second of setup**, not two seconds.

## 13 first-batch scripts — exact dialogue, with compact visual staging

### FM-V1-001 — Fox × Context
**0.0–0.5** Adult fox finds a blank map at a crossroads.
**0.5–1.0** Fox raises the pencil but points to two unknown destinations.
**1.0–5.0 / dialogue:** “Tell me where we're going before I draw the whole map.”
**Performance:** Clever, curious, not smug. **Point:** Ask for context before producing elaborate output.

### FM-V1-002 — Lion × Boundaries
**0.0–0.5** Adult lion stands beside a closed archive.
**0.5–1.0** A speaking trumpet is offered; the archive latch remains shut.
**1.0–5.0 / dialogue:** “Being allowed to speak doesn't mean you're allowed to open everything.”
**Performance:** Calm authority. **Point:** Permission to converse is not permission to access protected material.

### FM-V1-003 — Crow × Verification
**0.0–0.5** Adult crow spots a potion marked PERFECT on a shelf.
**0.5–1.0** Crow turns the bottle, revealing no proof of its claim.
**1.0–5.0 / dialogue:** “A confident label doesn't prove this potion does what it promises.”
**Performance:** Skeptical precision. **Point:** Check claims against evidence.
