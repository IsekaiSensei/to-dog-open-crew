### Owner meaning — do not collapse the references

The owner connects the first three references through **geometry → configuration → transformation**, not by importing *Hellraiser* IP into the game. The original seven-gem Page artwork is project material, while the puzzle box is third-party inspiration. The violet ring compositions express a fairy-origin narrative; do not substitute a random generated scene for the owner's exact artwork.

The owner also chose the wordplay **Paige → Page → Scroll → Book → World** because “Paige” sounds like “Page.” This is a thematic connection to the existing publishing vocabulary, **not** a statement that the public figure is a fictional in-world character, member of the crew, endorses the project, or provides lessons. The owner's separate wish to someday have a golf lesson with Paige remains a personal aspiration, not a task for Muse or an actual booking.

**Fairy appearance rule:** believable *adult* anatomy; never infantilized adults, chibi/cherub/childlike or age-ambiguous fairies. Do not infer permission to copy a real person's face, reproduce a photo, advertise an affiliation, or use third-party pictures as released project assets. Make original adult fairy designs rather than a Paige impersonation. Respect original agent art and actual licenses.
