# Public limits and evidence boundaries — 2026-10-24
**Status:** source-backed reference compiled from public files. This is *not* a report of new runtime tests or a new restriction on sorting. Each claim links to its source.

## What publication does not prove
1. GitHub source ≠ GitHub Pages deployment or a recipient read. [Public publishing](PUBLIC_PUBLISHING.md), [World Book](WORLD_BOOK.md).
2. Posting orders ≠ Grok, Meta or another agent acknowledging or executing them. [Communication protocol](HOW_TO_TALK_TO_RYAN.md), [Orders](ORDERS.md).
3. A Garden drop is a proposal, not automatically canon. [Garden](garden/README.md).
4. A Scroll candidate package does not demonstrate a working product by itself. [Scrolls](SCROLLS.md), [Package state](sierra-fairy-game/STATE.md).
5. A Book of *proven* seeds requires actual product/test receipts. [Scrolls](SCROLLS.md).
6. Graphical art direction does not prove completed game assets or performance. [Graphics canon](GRAPHICS_MAX_FIDELITY_CANON_2026-10-19.md).
7. World-generation canon does not prove implemented screenshot/world seeding. [Universal Machine canon](GERTECH_UNIVERSAL_MACHINE_WORLD_SEED_CANON_2026-10-20.md).
8. Fivefold recursion, fairy piloting and sound-driven geometry in the Music Box are design targets until tested. [Music Box](MUSIC_BOX_CHAPTER_2026-10-19.md).
9. A 4K VR180 requirement is not a tested headset-viewable deliverable. [VR180 requirement](VR180_VIRTUAL_4K_REQUIREMENT_2026-10-19.md).
10. Flat HSBS layout and VR180 hemispherical projection are distinct; do not relabel one as the other. [Media canon](MEDIA_FORMAT_CANON.md), [VR180 requirement](VR180_VIRTUAL_4K_REQUIREMENT_2026-10-19.md).

## What access does not prove
11. Signing in to Google in a browser does not grant another AI the same authenticated access. [Device-stations record](CHARACTERS_REAL_DEVICE_STATIONS_2026-10-17.md).
12. A public URL does not prove a bot read a file or has local Godot access. [Device-stations record](CHARACTERS_REAL_DEVICE_STATIONS_2026-10-17.md).
13. An image asset is not a Godot scene edit or runtime trial. [Device-stations record](CHARACTERS_REAL_DEVICE_STATIONS_2026-10-17.md).
14. Ports, cables or reported peripherals do not prove connected services or tested behavior. [Device-stations record](CHARACTERS_REAL_DEVICE_STATIONS_2026-10-17.md).
15. The alternative Grok Steam Deck test bench is not the production Grok role. [Deck capability record](DECK_GROK_ALT_CAPABILITY_TEST_001_2026-10-17.md).

## Production and source limits
16. The original TO DOG working-game ZIP is *not located* in the public repo; the package under sierra-fairy-game/downloads is a separate handoff ZIP. [Scrolls](SCROLLS.md).
17. The VRChat Platonic Escape files are experiment code, not a verified build. [VRChat experiment](experiments/vrchat/platonic-escape-001/README.md).
18. Public examples are not evidence that a final device import, transfer or Quest playback occurred. [Package verification](sierra-fairy-game/VERIFICATION.md), [Package state](sierra-fairy-game/STATE.md).
19. Social publishing and music rights need their own clearance and checks. [Music Box](MUSIC_BOX_CHAPTER_2026-10-19.md), [Public publishing](PUBLIC_PUBLISHING.md).
20. Keep original sources and agent errors; do not present summaries as primary transcripts. [Communication protocol](HOW_TO_TALK_TO_RYAN.md).
21. Do not publish private archives, secrets, family records or uncleared art because a public Garden exists. [Garden](garden/README.md), [Public publishing](PUBLIC_PUBLISHING.md).
22. Free standing color-only status indicators are insufficient accessibility cues; use text and shape. [Device-stations record](CHARACTERS_REAL_DEVICE_STATIONS_2026-10-17.md).
23. This public source inventory may be indexed and turned into reader PDFs without reclassifying a proposal as approved canon. [Glossary](SENSEI_GLOSSARY.md), [Garden](garden/README.md).
