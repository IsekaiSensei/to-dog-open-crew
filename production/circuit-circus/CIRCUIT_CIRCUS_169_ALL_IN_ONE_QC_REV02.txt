## TECHNICAL QC NOTES

1. **“Ground” is a circuit reference and return network**, not necessarily the literal earth; current needs a closed path.
2. **Fuses protect against overcurrent when correctly selected and coordinated with the circuit**; a fuse does not prevent every failure.
3. **A bare LED generally needs suitable current control**; do not show connecting one directly to a 9 V source without proper current limiting.
4. **An inductor opposes change in current**, not all change; its stored energy needs a safe path when switched.
5. **Op-amps and comparators have different typical configurations**; do not imply every op-amp is a proper comparator.
6. **555 and MCU lines need real, correct circuit connections**; a pulse appearing visually is not evidence of a working schematic.
7. **A robot requires mechanisms, sensors, motor drivers, control, power, and safety**, not merely an AI personality.
8. **Archived punchlines are verbatim satire**. Treat any oversimplification as a cue for the accurate visual explanation.
