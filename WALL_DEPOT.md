# WALL DEPOT

"Sir, this is Wall Depot."

This is the depot. The shelves hold the **structure** of a multi-agent build —
the handoff system, the review loops, the status words, the evidence labels —
with none of our personal information or universe data in it. Just the scaffolding,
stocked and labeled, ready to take home.

## What's on the shelves

- **JOIN_THE_FUTURE.md** — Sensei's dialogue: how to set up a multi-agent
  repository like this one. The whole structure, nothing personal.
- **The handoff packet format** — what every agent packet carries, in order.
- **The status words** — REQUESTED / CREATED / AVAILABLE / TRANSFERRED / READ-VERIFIED.
- **The evidence labels** — CANON / VERIFIED / INFERENCE / PROPOSAL / OPEN / RETIRED.

## Rules of the house

1. Take the structure. Leave our universe on the shelf — it's not yours.
2. Credit people. Preserve contributions. Agents help; they don't compete for ownership.
3. The commander is whoever runs it. That's you, in your repo.
4. Nothing here is anyone's personal data. Keep it that way.

## Why a depot

The fourth wall is the wall between the people making the thing and the people
watching it get made. A depot is where you go for parts. This is both:
the parts are the *methods*, and the wall stays up around everything personal.
