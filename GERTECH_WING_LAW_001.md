# GERTECH LAB — WING LAW 001

**System:** TO WORLD / GERTECH modular wing architecture  
**Authority:** Sensei's direct design instruction, 2026-10-26  
**Primary rule:** **WINGS ARE EQUIPMENT. FAIRIES ARE CHARACTERS. KEEP THEIR SOURCE DEFINITIONS SEPARATE.**

## 1. Independent assets

- Fairy identity, face, body, personality, and character lattice are independent of wing equipment.
- **Wing-only plates must not include fairy/humanoid renders or silhouettes.** Illustrate wings, panels, mounts, and interfaces alone.
- Any fairy may equip, change, exchange, or combine any compatible wing. Do not permanently assign wings to a persona, character name, element, or color.
- Attaching wings must not change a character's identity.

## 2. One universal modular architecture

All configurations derive from compatible gold-lattice structural members, modular solar/crystal panels, articulating hinges, load nodes, conduits, and equipment attachment interfaces. Wings can perform multiple functions and reconfigure without creating an entirely new character model. The game resolves geometry and interactions.

## 3. Five starter wing configurations ONLY

| ID | Function | Technical intent |
|---|---|---|
| W-01 | Atmospheric Lift | Aerodynamic lifting/steering surfaces; works with suitable airflow and propulsion. |
| W-02 | Stellar Collection | Photovoltaic and crystalline radiation harvesting, charging, energy routing. |
| W-03 | Stellar Sail | Reflective photon sailing and charged electric-sail interaction with stellar wind. |
| W-04 | Ion Propulsion | Stored-energy ion/plasma thrusters and space maneuvering with suitable propellant. |
| W-05 | Warp Transition | High-charge lattice resonance and fictional GERTECH wormhole transition. |

These are **equipment presets**, not separate fairies, five required upgrade stages, or five exclusive functions. Modes may be combined.

## 4. Combinatorics, not factorial

Five primary profiles A–E can be chosen for **three independently selectable game-defined positions** S1–S3. If repetition is allowed, this yields **5 × 5 × 5 = 5³ = 125 ordered loadout indices**.

- The three positions are **abstract runtime selection slots**, not fixed physical mount locations.
- The five initial pure references may be represented as AAA, BBB, CCC, DDD, EEE.
- Generate/evaluate the other 120 indices **in the game**. They are not 120 mandatory unique hand-authored or physically validated wing meshes.
- **5! = 120** counts arrangements of all five distinct modules used once. **2⁵ − 1 = 31** is the nonempty subset count. **5⁵ = 3125**, not 125. Do not interchange these counting models.

## 5. Runtime responsibility

Do not hand-render 125 character/wing combinations. Runtime generates suitable wing forms from shared module geometry, evaluates mount compatibility, energy routing, mechanical behavior, collision, and environmental operation, and collapses duplicate/unworkable candidates as needed.

## 6. Canon and presentation

This separation law is a current design directive; exploratory images do not independently establish canon. Generated performance values (mass, power, thrust, charging percentages, warp details) remain provisional until separately accepted.

**For all future wing designs: show wings alone. Fairies are separately rendered characters that pick up or equip the finished wing item.**
